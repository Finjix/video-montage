# V19 Wu Yue feedback-repair gate

This contract records Wu Yue feedback-repair rules. Output count and duration come only from the current work order; historical 40-output or 23–26 second requests are not defaults. It supplements V18 and overrides older batch-specific CTA exclusions.

## Non-negotiable batch rules

- Produce exactly the currently requested output count. Apply a duration range only when the current work order explicitly declares one; otherwise keep natural duration and do not slow, pad or add filler to meet a historical target. Before selection, prove enough valid ordered combinations for the requested batch and its declared diversity margin.
- Control repetition across exact candidate, text, opening, closing and complete route. Filename changes do not create diversity.
- Every output must remain source-spoken, visually coherent and celebrity-led. Never invent a requested phrase or replace an unavailable phrase with `so easy`.
- `so easy` is forbidden as a standalone segment or generic substitute. It may remain only inside one source-grounded, complete result utterance when the payoff tag is the natural end of that utterance; never cut the tag off and never isolate it.
- Every final shot is a complete useful unit of at least two seconds. Reject slogan fragments used only to reach duration or shot count.
- Build a visual-shot table. Native camera, angle or shot changes inside one source count as new visual shots. Longest minus shortest visual-shot duration must be no more than about three seconds.
- A supporting performer may appear only while Wu Yue is also visible. This applies to every frame, not only the opening.
- Reject generic status questions and assembled question/answer formats such as `你们这周收了几个幸存者`. A direct product question is usable only when Wu Yue is visible in the same shot and the selected answer actually resolves it.
- A standalone `我是无尽冬日明星玩家吴樾` opening is forbidden. It may be retained only when immediately followed by a first-person Wu Yue game-experience statement that makes the identity relevant.
- Remove `但/但是/不过/所以/然后/接着` at a candidate start unless the selected preceding speech supplies the exact dependency. Do not approve a connector because the remaining clause is grammatical.
- Allow source-spoken `视频下方进入`, `左下角点进去`, `无需下载点击即玩`, gift code and benefit lines in the middle or close while celebrity live action remains dominant. Continue to reject full-screen gameplay, end cards, QR-only screens and visual CTA without Wu Yue.

## Boundary and reuse rules

Review the exact selected interval at normal speed and inspect multiple native frames plus audio immediately before and after both cuts. `in/mid/out` stills alone are insufficient. Reject a leading finger flash, adjacent-face frame, residual `噔`, preceding phoneme, next-sentence phoneme or incomplete final character.

When one reused candidate fails, invalidate it for every dependent plan. Reuse becomes legal only after a new source interval, new boundary-window evidence and a new candidate fingerprint pass. A prior `pass` output is reusable only after this V19 recheck.
